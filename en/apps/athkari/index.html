Athkari

Quran, daily adhkar and prayer times in one app

Your daily companion for the Quran and remembrance: the full Mushaf with tafsir and recitation, morning and evening adhkar, the adhan on time, and salawat reminders. Completely free with no ads.

Features

The complete Mushaf

All 604 pages in Uthmani script, with surah and juz index, bookmarks, resume reading and full-text search.

4 tafsirs, offline

Al-Muyassar, Al-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir and Al-Jalalayn. Tap any ayah to read its tafsir.

12 reciters

Alafasy, Husary, Minshawi, Abdul Basit and more. Recitation keeps playing in the background with controls in the notification, and you can download it to listen offline.

Morning & evening adhkar

Plus 12+ categories: after prayer, sleep, waking up and duas, each with a counter and its source.

Prayer times & adhan

Pick the adhan voice from several muezzins and preview it first, a reminder before each prayer, and accurate times for your city.

Khatma plan & memorization

Choose how many days to finish the Quran and the app splits your daily portion, plus a memorization mode with repetition and hidden verses, and a daily checklist.

Salawat reminders

A spoken reminder every 10, 15 or 30 minutes or every hour — your choice.

Qibla direction

An accurate Qibla compass from anywhere.

Hijri calendar

Islamic occasions, with reminders for Monday/Thursday and the white days fasts.

Sadaqah jariyah

Names of your loved ones shown in the app with a dua and a request to read Al-Fatiha for them.

Group khatma

Complete the Quran together with family and friends as sadaqah jariyah: everyone claims a juz and sees the progress live.
